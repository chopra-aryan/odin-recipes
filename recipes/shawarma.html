Shawarma

Above image is a traditional lebanese shawarma made with lamb straight from lebanon

Ingredients:

  • 1 lb lamb
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 1/4 cup tahini
  • 1/4 cup lemon juice
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps:

  1. Marinate lamb by mixing yogurt and spices
  2. Leave it for few hours
  3. Grill lamb and gather your favorite veggies
  4. Grab a pita, put generous amount of garlic sauce on it, add your veggies and finally the lamb
  5. And Finally, take a big bite and Enjoy:)
